# Territory Scoring Criteria

Used in `SKILL.md` step 6. Score every territory that survives the
divergence audit (step 3) on all 5 criteria, 1 (weak) – 5 (strong), with
one sentence of rationale per score. An unscored or unjustified score
fails the quality gate.

## 1. Differentiation
Does this territory look, structurally, unlike (a) direct competitors and
(b) the other surviving territories in this run? Score against named
competitors from strategy where available, not a vague "feels different."

## 2. Strategic fit
Does the territory's 8-axis definition trace back to specific attributes
in `BRAND_STRATEGY.md` (positioning, promise, differentiation, audience)?
A territory that is distinctive but untraceable to strategy scores low
here even if it scores high on differentiation — divergence for its own
sake is not the goal.

## 3. Extensibility
Does the grammar hold up across the brand's actual expected surface area
(sub-brands, product lines, campaign variation, localization) without
needing a new rule invented for each new case? A territory that only works
for one hero asset scores low.

## 4. Production feasibility
Can this be built with the realistic budget, in-house skill, and
production timeline implied or stated in constraints? A brilliant
territory that requires bespoke 3D pipelines for a team with a static
design budget scores low here regardless of its other scores.

## 5. Cross-media behavior
Does the territory survive scale extremes (favicon/app-icon through
large-format) and, if motion is in scope, both static and animated
states? Score low if the territory only reads correctly at one specific
size or format (e.g. only works as a full-bleed hero image, breaks at
icon scale).


Selection (`SKILL.md` step 7) goes to the highest total that also has no
unresolved cliché flag from step 4 — not to the highest single criterion
in isolation (e.g. do not select purely for differentiation if it scores 1
on production feasibility with no path to fix that).
