# Worked Example — Radiotomography-Inspired Finance Product

## Input (abbreviated `BRAND_STRATEGY.md`)
- **Category:** B2B financial forecasting software for CFOs at mid-market
  companies.
- **Positioning:** "The forecast that shows its work" — against
  competitors that present a single confident number, this product exposes
  the layered assumptions beneath every projection.
- **Differentiation:** Transparency of method, not accuracy claims (every
  competitor claims accuracy). Users distrust black-box forecasts after
  being burned by them.
- **Audience:** Skeptical, numerate CFOs who want to interrogate a number,
  not be reassured by one.
- **Reference supplied:** "radiotomography" (medical/industrial scan
  imagery) as an inspiration word, with the note "we like that it shows
  what's underneath, not what it looks like on the outside."

## Step 1 — tension derivation (excerpt)
| Attribute | Tension | Rules out |
|---|---|---|
| "Shows its work" | Legibility of *process* vs. a single hero result | A dashboard whose primary visual is one big confident number |
| "Skeptical, numerate audience" | Density tolerance vs. approachable minimalism | Airy, sparse marketing-style UI that hides the numbers being fewer/friendlier |

## Step 5 — reference-to-principle mapping
Radiotomography → principle: *translucency reveals structure otherwise
hidden*, transferred to color behavior (layered opacity showing an
assumption "beneath" the headline forecast) and motion (progressive reveal
on interaction, echoing a scan pass) — not literal X-ray colors or medical
imagery.

## Territories generated (summary; full 8-axis + scores in
`VISUAL_TERRITORIES.md` pattern)
1. **"Cross-Section"** (selected) — layered translucent planes showing
   assumption-stacks beneath a headline number; monospace-adjacent
   numerals for structural precision, humanist sans for narrative text;
   color reserved for confidence-interval bands only; progressive-reveal
   motion on drill-down; dense by default, no "friendly whitespace"
   softening.
2. **"Ledger Grain"** (runner-up) — paper-ledger materiality, rag-edge
   tables, ink-density used to encode certainty instead of translucency;
   diverges from Territory 1 on materiality, color behavior, and image
   language (paper texture vs. flat digital plane).
3. **"Signal Field"** (discarded — collided with Territory 1 on 5/8 axes
   in first draft: same composition, color behavior, density; rebuilt
   attempt still landed on the same underlying idea with a different
   accent color, so it was dropped rather than kept as false variety).

## Selection
Territory 1, "Cross-Section" — highest strategic-fit and differentiation
score; no unresolved cliché flags (checked against "finance = navy + line
chart" default — this territory deliberately avoids a single hero line
chart, satisfying the flag check rather than triggering it).

## Output excerpt — `APPROVED_VISUAL_DIRECTION.md`
> **Color logic — core rule:** Color never decorates; it exists only to
> encode confidence level in a layered assumption stack. Resting UI is
> near-monochrome ink-on-paper digital surface. This rules out any
> per-section brand-color theming.
>
> **Compositional principle:** Every forecast surface shows at least one
> layer beneath the headline number by default — never a single
> unexplained figure, even in a compact/widget view.

## Output excerpt — `VISUAL_ANTI_PATTERNS.md`
> Ruled out: navy + single green "trust" accent (category default for
> finance — flagged, not overridden, because this brand's differentiation
> is method-transparency, not reassurance-signaling, which the default
> palette actively undercuts).
> Ruled out: single confident hero number/line-chart as primary visual —
> directly contradicts "shows its work" positioning.
