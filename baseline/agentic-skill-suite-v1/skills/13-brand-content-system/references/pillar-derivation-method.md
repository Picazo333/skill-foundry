# Reference: deriving content pillars from strategy

Used in `SKILL.md` steps 2–3 (journey map, pillar derivation). This is the
method that keeps pillars traceable instead of a generic content checklist.

## The test a pillar must pass
A candidate is a valid pillar only if you can complete this sentence with a
specific line from `BRAND_STRATEGY.md` or `VERBAL_IDENTITY.md`:

> "This pillar exists because [strategy claim/differentiator/proof point]
> answers [journey stage]'s question for this specific audience."

If the blank can only be filled with something true of almost any brand in
the category ("we want to be helpful", "people like behind-the-scenes"), it
is not a pillar — it is a generic content category wearing a pillar's name.

## Step-by-step
1. **List the journey questions** (attention/trust/intent/conversion/
   retention — see `SKILL.md` step 2). These are the same five questions for
   every brand; what differs is the answer.
2. **List strategy's answer to each question**, pulled verbatim or
   near-verbatim from `BRAND_STRATEGY.md`: the positioning statement, named
   differentiators, proof points, audience pains/objections the strategy
   explicitly names.
3. **Pair question → answer → candidate pillar.** Name the candidate in the
   brand's own vocabulary (check `VERBAL_IDENTITY.md` for the term the brand
   itself uses for this idea — don't default to a marketing-generic label).
4. **Merge duplicates.** If two journey stages are answered by the same
   underlying claim, that's one pillar serving two stages, not two pillars.
5. **Run the test above on every survivor.** Cut anything that fails it.
6. **Check capacity fit.** More than 5 pillars is a signal either that
   merging in step 4 was incomplete, or that the system is being designed
   for aspirational scope rather than what step 7 of `SKILL.md` (cadence
   from real capacity) can actually sustain. Prefer fewer, better-traced
   pillars over broad generic coverage.

## Worked contrast
- **Generic (reject):** Pillar = "Educational tips." Fails the test — any
  brand in any category could claim this.
- **Traced (accept):** Pillar = "The [proprietary method name]." Test:
  "This pillar exists because [the brand's named proprietary method,
  cited as the core differentiator in BRAND_STRATEGY.md] answers Trust's
  question ('can I believe what they claim?') by showing the mechanism, not
  just asserting it works." This is specific to the brand and would not
  transfer to a competitor without also stealing their method.

## When strategy doesn't answer a stage
Not every brand's strategy will have a distinct answer for every journey
stage — that's a real finding, not a gap to paper over. Note it in
`CONTENT_SYSTEM.md`'s journey map as "no distinct pillar for this stage;
served by [pillar] at reduced strength" rather than inventing a pillar to
fill the cell.
