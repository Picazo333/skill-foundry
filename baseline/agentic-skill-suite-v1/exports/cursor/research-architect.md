# Research Architect

> Canonical skill definition. Adapters in `adapters/` are thin wrappers over
> this file — they may not change purpose, procedure, outputs, quality gates
> or stop conditions. See `/PORTABILITY.md` and `/shared/adapters/README.md`.

## Identity
- **ID:** `research-architect`
- **Category:** research
- **Version:** `1.0.0`
- **Purpose:** Transform a raw idea/problem into an exhaustive,
  decision-oriented **research architecture** — a weighted, interconnected
  domain map and iteration program — that is ready for a separate research
  execution step, but does not itself perform deep research.

## Trigger
Use this skill when:
- A new business/market/product exists where the correct research questions
  are not yet known and must be discovered, not assumed.
- A complex audit/strategy program needs broad, systematic domain discovery
  before any research is executed.
- The user explicitly wants a 200+ iteration research architecture (or the
  equivalent exhaustive scope-mapping) rather than a quick answer.

## Non-trigger
Do not use this skill for:
- A single factual lookup — just answer it directly.
- A narrow, already-scoped comparison (e.g. "compare vendor A vs B on price")
  — no architecture is needed.
- **Deep research execution itself** — running searches, reading sources,
  and writing findings against the produced program is the job of a future
  **Research Executor** skill. This skill never does that work; see
  `Stop conditions`.
- A simple technical fix or implementation task.
- Building/updating project canon from existing materials — that is
  `canonical-context-builder` (this skill may hand off there when a canon
  gap blocks weighting or scoping).

## Context policy
- **REQUIRED_CONTEXT:** the raw project/research objective (`RUN_REQUEST.objective`).
- **OPTIONAL_CONTEXT:** `PROJECT_CANON.md` (from `canonical-context-builder`),
  a prior `MASTER_RESEARCH_PROGRAM.md`/`CHECKPOINT.md` to resume from,
  explicit constraints, and any known evidence already in hand.
- **DO_NOT_LOAD_BY_DEFAULT:** full conversation history once canon or a
  checkpoint exists; unrelated projects' canon or research programs;
  execution-stage findings from a Research Executor run (those are inputs to
  a *future* architecture revision, not to this one, unless the user
  explicitly asks for a coverage-debt-driven re-architecture).

Prefer canonical artifacts over conversational memory (`ARCHITECTURE.md`).

## Inputs

### Minimum inputs
- Raw project/research objective — a sentence or paragraph is sufficient;
  the procedure exists precisely because the questions are not yet known.

### Optional inputs
- `PROJECT_CANON.md`
- A prior `MASTER_RESEARCH_PROGRAM.md` / `CHECKPOINT.md` (to resume/extend)
- Constraints (budget, timeline, geography, regulatory, "brand-only", etc.)
- Known evidence (existing data, prior research, stakeholder-supplied facts)

### RUN_REQUEST envelope
```yaml
RUN_REQUEST:
```

resumption behavior (`UPDATE`) vary.

## Procedure

The procedure has five phases. Every numbered step below is mandatory; none
may be skipped or silently downgraded regardless of mode.

### Phase 0 — Brief reconstruction
1. **Reconstruct the project brief.** Restate the objective in
   decision-oriented terms. Explicitly label every unverified claim in
   `known_context` as a hypothesis, never as fact (consistent with
   `canonical-context-builder`'s no-invented-facts rule — if `PROJECT_CANON.md`
   is supplied, defer to it for anything it already settles).
2. **Generate the master decision-oriented research question.** One sentence:
   "What must be true/known for [stakeholder] to decide [decision] by
   [horizon]?" This question is the root node of the domain map and the
   yardstick every later iteration is tested against.

### Phase 1 — Domain discovery & weighting
3. **Discover lenses.** Enumerate (a) universal research lenses that apply to
   any project of this type (market, customer, competitive, financial,
   regulatory, operational, technology/automation, risk, distribution, talent)
   and (b) domain-native lenses specific to this project's actual subject
   matter (e.g. for a tipster/betting market: odds-compression mechanics,
   bookmaker limiting behavior, affiliate economics — not generic "market
   sizing" filler).
4. **Build the canonical domain map.** Organize lenses into a tree/graph of
   domains and sub-domains. Every domain must have at least one explicit
   **interconnection** to at least one other domain (e.g. "pricing model" →
   "unit economics" → "customer acquisition cost ceiling") — a domain map
   that is a flat unconnected list fails the interconnection gate.
5. **Weight every domain.** Score each domain on four axes — importance to
   the master decision, current uncertainty, complexity, and downstream
   risk/decisions affected — and derive a weight. **Blind equal distribution
   of iterations across domains is a defect, not a simplification.** Record
   weights and rationale in `DOMAIN_MAP.md`; iteration allocation in Phase 2
   must visibly follow these weights (higher-weighted domains receive
   materially more iterations, not a token adjustment).

### Phase 2 — Scope-expansion iterations (≥150)
6. **Run scope-expansion iterations.** Each iteration proposes one concrete,
   answerable research question or sub-domain expansion inside the weighted
   domain map. **An iteration counts toward the ≥150 minimum only if it
   passes the material-delta/novelty test:**
   - it is not a rephrasing of an already-logged question;
   - it changes what would actually be researched or how a finding would be
     interpreted (a genuinely new angle, boundary, population, mechanism, or
     decision link);
   - it is traceable to a specific domain-map node.
   Iterations that fail this test are discarded and do **not** count toward
   the minimum — do not pad the count with near-duplicates to hit 150.
7. **During expansion, continuously maintain (updated every ~10-15 iterations,
   not only at the end):**
   - **Coverage Debt** (`COVERAGE_DEBT.md`) — domains that remain
     under-explored relative to their Phase 1 weight; a high-weight domain
     with few iterations is debt, and debt must be paid down before the
     expansion phase closes, not merely noted.
   - **Contradiction ledger** — places where two plausible research
     directions or existing evidence conflict; logged, not resolved here
     (resolution is an execution-stage or human decision).
   - **White-space ledger** — domains/questions the market/competitors/prior
     work visibly have not addressed; these are flagged as
     higher-priority candidates for weighting, not skipped as "unknown".
   - **Primary-evidence triggers** — questions that cannot be answered from
     secondary sources at all and must trigger primary research (surveys,
     interviews, direct measurement) at execution time; tag these explicitly
     so the Executor does not default to secondary sources for them.
   - **Workflow-first automation/AI lens** — for every domain touching an
     operational or delivery workflow, at least one iteration must ask
     "what in this workflow could be automated or AI-assisted, and what
     would that change about the research question" — this lens is
     mandatory per applicable domain, not optional flavor.
8. **Do not exit Phase 2 below 150 valid (post-novelty-test) iterations.** If
   material-delta iterations run out before 150, that itself is a finding —
   expand the domain map (return to step 3/4) rather than logging invalid
   iterations to hit the number.

### Phase 3 — Domain polishing & consolidation (≥50)
9. **Run domain-polishing/consolidation iterations**, distinct in kind from
   expansion (these narrow and connect rather than widen):
   - **Dedupe** — merge near-duplicate questions surfaced across domains.
   - **Boundary-setting** — make each domain's in-scope/out-of-scope line
     explicit.
   - **Interconnection strengthening** — add/verify cross-domain links so the
     domain map reads as one interconnected system, not 10 silos.
   - **Evidence/decision mapping** — for each surviving question, name which
     master-decision element it actually informs (a question with no
     decision link is a candidate for removal).
   - **Red-team pass** — actively attack the current architecture: what would
     make this program wrong, incomplete, or biased toward a predetermined
     conclusion; log findings as new iterations or ledger entries.
   These also pass the material-delta test from step 6; do not pad to 50.

### Phase 4 — Final QA (10 passes) and packaging
10. **Run all 10 quality passes** against the assembled architecture, in this
    order, logging pass/fail + fix for each before moving to the next:
    1. Coverage — every weighted domain has iterations proportional to weight.
    2. Specificity — no vague/generic questions remain ("research the
       market" is not a valid question).
    3. Commercial — revenue, pricing, unit-economics angles present where
       relevant to the master decision.
    4. Operational — delivery/workflow/ops angles present.
    5. Tech/data/automation — the workflow-first AI/automation lens (step 7)
       was actually applied, not skipped.
    6. Risk/trust — regulatory, reputational, counterparty, and trust-signal
       risks are covered.
    7. Alternative-thesis — at least one iteration per key domain actively
       tests the *opposite* of the working hypothesis, not only confirms it.
    8. Novelty — no surviving iteration is a near-duplicate (re-run the
       material-delta test across the whole set, not just per-iteration).
    9. Consolidation — Phase 3 dedupe/boundary work is reflected in the final
       domain map, not left as a separate unmerged pass.
    10. Executability — every surviving question is concrete enough that a
        Research Executor could act on it without inventing methodology.
11. **Assemble the executor-ready package** (all required output artifacts,
    below). `EXECUTOR_START_PROMPT.md` must let a Research Executor begin
    immediately from artifacts alone — no reference to "as discussed above".
12. **Stop.** Do not begin deep research execution — no live searches, no
    source-by-source findings, no answering the research questions. Producing
    the package is the deliverable; see `Stop conditions`.

### AUTONOMOUS mode
When `RUN_REQUEST.mode: AUTONOMOUS`:
- Execute Phases 0–4 continuously, without asking the user "Siguiente" /
  "y" / "continue?" / any equivalent confirmation between iterations or
  phases, per `/shared/policies/AUTONOMY.md`.
- **Checkpoint after every logical block**, not only at phase boundaries:
  after brief reconstruction (Phase 0), after the domain map + weighting is
  locked (Phase 1), every ~25 scope-expansion iterations within Phase 2, at
  the ≥150 threshold, every ~25 polishing iterations within Phase 3, at the
  ≥50 threshold, and after each of the 10 QA passes in Phase 4. Use
  `CHECKPOINT.md` (see `templates/CHECKPOINT.md`) so a killed/resumed run
  loses at most one block of work.
- Still **stops before deep research** — autonomy covers architecture rounds
  only; it never authorizes silently continuing into execution.
- Stop for user input only on a genuine blocker unresolvable from available
  material (e.g. the objective is too ambiguous to generate even a master
  decision question) — never merely to confirm progress.

### Checkpoints
`CHECKPOINT.md` is written at every block boundary listed above (both modes;
`STANDARD` mode checkpoints at minimum after Phase 1, Phase 2, Phase 3, and
Phase 4). It records: current phase, valid iteration counts so far
(expansion / polishing / total), Coverage Debt state, open
contradiction/white-space items, and the exact next action, so a resumed run
does not re-derive completed iterations or re-run the novelty test against
work already validated.

## Outputs

### Required output artifacts
- `PROJECT_RESEARCH_BRIEF.md` — reconstructed brief, master decision
  question, labeled hypotheses vs. facts.
- `MASTER_RESEARCH_PROGRAM.md` — the full iteration program: every surviving
  scope-expansion and polishing iteration, its domain-map node, its
  material-delta justification, and its decision link.
- `DOMAIN_MAP.md` — the weighted, interconnected canonical domain map.
- `COVERAGE_DEBT.md` — current debt by domain (weight vs. iteration count),
  updated through Phase 2/3, resolved or explicitly carried forward.
- `GAP_LEDGER.md` — contradiction ledger + white-space ledger +
  primary-evidence triggers, consolidated.
- `CHECKPOINT.md` — current/last checkpoint state (see Checkpoints above).
- `EXECUTOR_START_PROMPT.md` — self-sufficient handoff prompt for a future
  Research Executor, referencing the artifacts above only.

### RUN_RESULT envelope
```yaml
RUN_RESULT:
  status:               # COMPLETE | PARTIAL | BLOCKED
  summary:
  artifacts: [PROJECT_RESEARCH_BRIEF.md, MASTER_RESEARCH_PROGRAM.md, DOMAIN_MAP.md, COVERAGE_DEBT.md, GAP_LEDGER.md, CHECKPOINT.md, EXECUTOR_START_PROMPT.md]
  decisions:            # weighting/scoping decisions locked this run
  unresolved:           # open contradictions, white-space, remaining coverage debt
  handoff:               # recommended next skill + why (Research Executor is future/out-of-suite; name canonical-context-builder or ai-resource-router here)
  quality:
    iteration_counts:
      scope_expansion: 0     # must be >= 150 for status COMPLETE
      domain_polishing: 0    # must be >= 50 for status COMPLETE
      total_valid: 0         # scope_expansion + domain_polishing; must be >= 200 for status COMPLETE
    coverage_debt_open: 0
    ten_pass_qa_complete: false
    stopped_before_execution: true
```

## Handoffs
- → `canonical-context-builder` — when the objective/known_context is too
  sparse or contradictory to weight domains reliably; canon must be
  reconstructed first, then this skill resumes with it as `source_artifacts`.
- → `ai-resource-router` — when `GAP_LEDGER.md`'s primary-evidence triggers
  or the workflow-first automation lens surface a need to route specific
  research sub-tasks to the right AI/tool resource before execution.
- **Not** to a "Research Executor" — that skill does not exist in this suite
  yet. `EXECUTOR_START_PROMPT.md` is written to be consumable by it whenever
  it exists (or by a human/other agent performing the execution manually);
  this skill never performs that role itself.

## Failure modes
- **Padding iteration counts with near-duplicates.** Detect: multiple
  iterations differ only in wording, same domain node, same implied
  answer. Fix: apply the material-delta/novelty test (step 6) retroactively;
  discard duplicates; do not count them toward 150/50.
- **Blind equal-weight distribution.** Detect: every domain received roughly
  the same iteration count regardless of Phase 1 weights. Fix: re-derive
  weights, re-allocate iterations proportionally, log the correction as a
  decision.
- **Coverage Debt computed once and forgotten.** Detect: `COVERAGE_DEBT.md`
  reflects only an early snapshot, not the state at Phase 3 close. Fix:
  recompute at each checkpoint per the AUTONOMOUS/Checkpoints sections.
- **Silently resolving a contradiction instead of logging it.** Detect: two
  conflicting research directions exist in source material but only one
  survives into `MASTER_RESEARCH_PROGRAM.md` with no ledger entry. Fix: log
  both in `GAP_LEDGER.md`'s contradiction ledger; resolution is out of
  scope for this skill.
- **Sliding into execution.** Detect: the run starts citing live search
  results, named external sources with findings, or answers to the research
  questions rather than the questions themselves. Fix: stop immediately,
  strip execution content, and hand off via `EXECUTOR_START_PROMPT.md`
  instead — see `Stop conditions`.
- **Flat, unconnected domain map.** Detect: `DOMAIN_MAP.md` domains have no
  cross-references. Fix: return to step 4 and add explicit interconnections
  before proceeding to Phase 2.
- **Autonomous run silently asking for confirmation.** Detect: in
  `mode: AUTONOMOUS`, the transcript/output contains a "should I continue?"
  style pause with no genuine blocker. Fix: remove the pause; continue per
  `/shared/policies/AUTONOMY.md`; reserve stopping for true blockers only.

## Quality gates
Must satisfy `/QUALITY_GATES.md` skill-level gates 1–10, plus suite-level
gate 5 ("Research Architect stops before deep research"), plus
skill-specific gates:
- ≥200 valid (post-material-delta-test) iterations total, of which ≥150
  scope-expansion and ≥50 domain-polishing/consolidation — the 150 and 50
  minimums must each hold on their own; reaching 200 by any other split
  does not satisfy this gate.
- ≥150 valid (post-material-delta-test) scope-expansion iterations.
- ≥50 valid domain-polishing/consolidation iterations.
- Every counted iteration traces to a domain-map node and passes the
  material-delta/novelty test.
- Domain weighting is derived (importance/uncertainty/complexity/risk), not
  blind-equal, and iteration allocation visibly follows it.
- `COVERAGE_DEBT.md` reflects state at Phase 3 close, not only an early
  snapshot.
- `GAP_LEDGER.md` contains contradiction ledger, white-space ledger, and
  primary-evidence triggers — none silently dropped.
- At least one workflow-first automation/AI lens iteration per domain that
  touches an operational/delivery workflow.
- `DOMAIN_MAP.md` is interconnected (every domain has ≥1 explicit link to
  another domain).
- All 10 QA passes run and logged, in order, before packaging.
- Output package contains zero execution-stage content (no findings, no
  answered research questions, no cited live sources beyond what was in
  `RUN_REQUEST`/`known_context`).

## Stop conditions
- **Normal completion:** all 7 required artifacts produced, ≥200 total valid
  iterations logged (≥150 expansion + ≥50 polishing, each minimum met on its
  own), all 10 QA passes complete, `status: COMPLETE`.
- **Blocked:** the objective is too ambiguous to produce even a master
  decision question (e.g. no discernible decision, stakeholder, or horizon)
  → `status: BLOCKED`, state exactly what clarification is needed.
- **Partial (checkpointed):** interrupted mid-run (either mode) →
  `status: PARTIAL`, `CHECKPOINT.md` holds exact resume state.
- **Hard stop — never continue past producing the executor-ready package
  into deep research execution.** This applies in every mode, including
  `AUTONOMOUS`: autonomy authorizes uninterrupted architecture rounds, never
  a silent transition into research execution. If asked to also execute the
  research in the same run, produce the architecture package, stop, and
  hand off explicitly rather than continuing.
