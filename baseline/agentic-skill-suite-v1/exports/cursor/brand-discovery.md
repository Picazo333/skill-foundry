# Brand Discovery

> Canonical skill definition. Adapters in `adapters/` are thin wrappers over
> this file — they may not change purpose, procedure, outputs, quality gates
> or stop conditions. See `/PORTABILITY.md` and `/shared/adapters/README.md`.

## Identity
- **ID:** `brand-discovery`
- **Category:** brand
- **Version:** `1.0.0`
- **Purpose:** Turn fragmented founder/client/business information into a
  canonical brand discovery brief that separates FACTS, CLAIMS, ASPIRATIONS,
  HYPOTHESES and NON-NEGOTIABLES, and maps references/anti-references by the
  underlying quality they signal — without deciding positioning or making any
  visual/verbal choice.

## Trigger
Use this skill when:
- A brand is starting from scratch or being repositioned and no canonical
  discovery brief yet exists.
- Client/founder discovery data exists (interviews, briefs, decks, notes,
  analytics, existing brand materials) and needs to be turned into a single
  trustworthy foundation.
- `brand-strategy` (or another downstream brand skill) needs a foundation and
  none exists yet, or the existing one is stale against new source material.

## Non-trigger
Do not use this skill for:
- **Final positioning or a chosen brand strategy** (target audience decision,
  positioning statement, competitive stance) — that is `brand-strategy`,
  which consumes this skill's brief.
- **Logo design, visual identity, or any visual-system decision** — that is
  `brand-visual-direction` / `brand-identity-system`.
- **Generic moodboard generation** disconnected from stated
  references/anti-references — this skill maps references the source
  material actually names or implies, it does not generate new ones.
- **General project canon** (timeline, technical decisions, non-brand facts)
  — that is `canonical-context-builder`; use its output as input here when
  available instead of re-deriving it.
- **Deep external market/competitor research** — if discovery surfaces a gap
  that needs primary research beyond the supplied material, hand off to
  `research-architect` rather than researching it inline.

## Context policy
- **REQUIRED_CONTEXT:** raw client/project/business context (at minimum:
  what the business does, for whom, and why discovery is being run now).
- **OPTIONAL_CONTEXT:** founder/stakeholder interviews, analytics, competitor
  notes, existing identity/brand materials, named references and
  anti-references, a prior `PROJECT_CANON.md` from `canonical-context-builder`.
- **DO_NOT_LOAD_BY_DEFAULT:** full conversation history once source
  artifacts exist (use the artifacts, not the chat that produced them);
  other brands' discovery briefs or reference maps (cross-client
  contamination); downstream skills' outputs (`brand-strategy` positioning
  drafts, visual moodboards) — discovery must not be shaped by decisions that
  haven't been made yet.

Prefer canonical artifacts over conversational memory (`ARCHITECTURE.md`).

## Inputs

### Minimum inputs
- Raw client/project/business context: what the business does, who it
  (thinks it) serves, and why discovery is happening now.

### Optional inputs
- Founder/stakeholder interviews (transcripts, notes, voice-note summaries).
- Analytics (site, social, sales) showing actual customer behavior.
- Competitor notes.
- Existing brand/identity materials (deck, style guide, prior logo, website
  copy).
- Named references and anti-references ("we like X", "we are NOT Y").
- Prior `PROJECT_CANON.md` (from `canonical-context-builder`), if this brand
  project already has one.
- Prior `BRAND_DISCOVERY_BRIEF.md` / `DISCOVERY_GAPS.md`, if `mode: UPDATE`.

## Procedure
Numbered, executable steps. Each step should be concrete enough that two
independent runs against the same inputs converge on materially the same
brief.

1. **Inventory.** List every source artifact. For each, note what dimension
   of context it covers: business (what/how it makes money), offer
   (product/service specifics), customer (who buys, who uses), market
   (competitors, category norms), founder (personal motivations, red lines,
   taste), operational (constraints on budget, timeline, team, legal). Flag
   any dimension with zero source coverage — it feeds step 6, not an
   invented answer.

2. **Classify every extracted statement into exactly one bucket.** Do not
   leave an extracted item unclassified and do not put the same statement in
   two buckets. Use `references/fact-vs-opinion-rubric.md` for the
   FACTS/CLAIMS boundary, which is the one buckets most often get confused:
   - **FACTS** — independently verifiable or structurally undeniable (years
     in business, number of locations, price point, legal structure,
     measured analytics, verbatim customer quotes).
   - **CLAIMS** — a stakeholder's assertion about the business or its
     standing that is not independently verified ("we're the most trusted
     clinic in the city", "our customers are obsessed with us"). Record the
     claim AND who made it; do not silently promote a claim to a fact.
   - **ASPIRATIONS** — explicitly forward-looking: how the business *wants*
     to be perceived or *wants* to become, stated as not-yet-true ("we want
     to feel like the Aesop of dental care").
   - **HYPOTHESES** — the skill's own pattern-based inferences that no
     source explicitly stated (e.g., inferring a likely target segment from
     analytics + offer, without anyone having said so). Always tag these as
     `hypothesis, unconfirmed` — they are candidates for `brand-strategy` to
     test, not conclusions.
   - **NON-NEGOTIABLES** — hard constraints stated by the client/founder or
     imposed externally (legal/regulatory naming limits, a founder's flat
     refusal of a direction, a fixed budget or timeline, an element of the
     existing brand that must survive).

3. **Extract the four discovery axes.** From the classified material, pull
   out and state explicitly, each traced to its source:
   - Customer language — words/phrases customers themselves use (from
     reviews, quotes, support tickets, interviews), not marketing language
     about them.
   - Desired perception — how the business wants to be seen (drawn from
     ASPIRATIONS + founder CLAIMS).
   - Current perception — how the business is actually seen today, if any
     evidence exists (reviews, analytics, competitor comparison, direct
     customer language) — distinct from desired perception; do not merge
     the two.
   - Business objective — the concrete business reason discovery/rebrand is
     happening now (grow revenue, enter new segment, fix a trust problem,
     support a fundraise, etc.), stated as a FACT or CLAIM, not inferred
     unless no source states it (then a flagged HYPOTHESIS).

4. **Map references and anti-references by underlying quality, not
   superficial similarity.** For every named reference (brand, competitor,
   piece of design, tone of voice example) and anti-reference, do not record
   "reference: Brand X" alone — name the *specific quality* it was invoked
   for (e.g., "confident minimalism", "irreverent but warm humor",
   "clinical/cold — avoid") and cite who named it and in what words. Two
   references to different brands that were invoked for the same underlying
   quality should be grouped together in `REFERENCE_MAP.md`; a reference and
   an anti-reference that name the *same* quality from opposite directions
   (e.g. "want warmth like X" / "don't want sterile like Y") are the
   clearest signal in the brief — call that out explicitly, it is not a
   coincidence to bury.

5. **Identify contradictions.** Scan FACTS/CLAIMS/ASPIRATIONS/NON-NEGOTIABLES
   and the reference map together for tensions the source material has not
   resolved — canonical examples: premium vs. accessible, expressive vs.
   clinical, exclusive vs. mass-market, heritage vs. disruptive. A
   contradiction is anything where two stated positions cannot both be true
   of the same brand without a strategic choice between them. Do NOT resolve
   it — that choice belongs to `brand-strategy`. Record it verbatim with both
   sides and their sources in `BRAND_DISCOVERY_BRIEF.md`'s Contradictions
   section.

6. **Identify discovery gaps — materiality filter.** A missing piece of
   information is a *gap* worth logging in `DISCOVERY_GAPS.md` only if it
   would materially block one of `brand-strategy`'s core decisions:
   target audience/segment, positioning axis, competitive frame, or the
   business objective itself. If a dimension is missing but doesn't block
   any of those (e.g., "we don't know the founder's favorite color") it is
   omitted, not logged — `DISCOVERY_GAPS.md` must stay decision-relevant, not
   a list of every unknown thing. For each material gap, state exactly what
   question would close it and who could answer it.

7. **Assemble the canonical outputs.** Build `BRAND_DISCOVERY_BRIEF.md`
   (business/offer/customer/market/founder/operational inventory +
   FACTS/CLAIMS/ASPIRATIONS/HYPOTHESES/NON-NEGOTIABLES + the four discovery
   axes + Contradictions), `REFERENCE_MAP.md` (references/anti-references
   grouped by underlying quality), and `DISCOVERY_GAPS.md` (material gaps
   only, each with a closing question and an owner).

8. **UPDATE mode.** If `mode: UPDATE` and a prior brief exists, diff new
   source material against it: only rewrite sections the new material
   actually changes; note in the brief what changed, from what source, and
   whether it resolves a previously logged gap or contradiction (mark it
   resolved) or introduces a new one.

9. **Stop before strategy or visual work.** Do not draft a positioning
   statement, do not choose between contradictory directions, do not
   describe a visual direction or moodboard, do not write brand voice
   guidelines. The brief documents what exists and what's unresolved; it
   does not decide anything `brand-strategy` is responsible for deciding.

### Checkpoints
For discovery runs with many source artifacts (multiple long interview
transcripts, analytics exports, existing brand decks), checkpoint after step
2 (all statements classified, before axis extraction) and after step 6
(gaps identified, before final assembly), recording counts of items
classified per bucket and gaps identified so a resumed run does not
re-classify from scratch. Use `/shared/contracts/checkpoint.schema.json`.

## Outputs

### Required output artifacts
- `BRAND_DISCOVERY_BRIEF.md` — the full classified inventory, the four
  discovery axes, and the unresolved Contradictions section.
- `DISCOVERY_GAPS.md` — material, decision-blocking gaps only, each with a
  closing question and an owner.
- `REFERENCE_MAP.md` — references/anti-references grouped by underlying
  quality, with source attribution.

## Handoffs
- → `brand-strategy` — the normal next stage (`routing-graph.json`:
  `brand-discovery → brand-strategy`, purpose "discovery brief"). Passes all
  three artifacts; `brand-strategy` resolves the Contradictions section and
  decides positioning — this skill never does.
- → `research-architect` — when a `DISCOVERY_GAPS.md` entry needs primary
  market/competitor research beyond what the supplied material contains, not
  answerable from source material alone.
- → `canonical-context-builder` — when discovery surfaces project-level
  facts (timeline, non-brand decisions) that belong in general project canon
  rather than the brand discovery brief.

## Failure modes
- **Inventing a fact or customer insight not in the source material.**
  Detect: a `BRAND_DISCOVERY_BRIEF.md` entry has no traceable source. Fix:
  either it is a HYPOTHESIS explicitly tagged `unconfirmed`, or it is
  removed.
- **Promoting a founder CLAIM to a FACT.** Detect: an unverified stakeholder
  assertion appears under FACTS. Fix: move to CLAIMS with attribution to the
  stakeholder who made it.
- **Merging desired perception and current perception.** Detect: the brief
  states one "perception" without distinguishing what the business wants vs.
  what evidence shows today. Fix: split into the two axes per step 3, and if
  no evidence exists for current perception, say so explicitly rather than
  defaulting to the desired one.
- **Resolving a contradiction instead of logging it.** Detect: the brief
  picks a side of a premium-vs-accessible-style tension without that being a
  quoted decision from a source. Fix: move it back to the Contradictions
  section, unresolved, for `brand-strategy` to decide.
- **Gap-list bloat.** Detect: `DISCOVERY_GAPS.md` lists unknowns that don't
  block any `brand-strategy` decision (trivia, nice-to-knows). Fix: apply
  the materiality filter (step 6) and drop anything that doesn't block
  target audience, positioning axis, competitive frame, or business
  objective.
- **Reference-mapping by surface similarity.** Detect: a reference is logged
  as "brand X" with no stated underlying quality, or grouped with another
  reference only because they're in the same industry. Fix: re-derive the
  quality each reference was actually invoked for (step 4) and regroup by
  that.
- **Doing strategy or visual work inside discovery.** Detect: the brief
  contains a positioning statement, a chosen direction, a color/typography
  suggestion, or brand voice guidelines. Fix: remove it; note it as a
  question for `brand-strategy` / `brand-visual-direction` instead of
  answering it here.

## Quality gates
Must satisfy `/QUALITY_GATES.md` skill-level gates 1–10, plus the
skill-specific gates from `SPEC.md`:
- Facts/hypotheses (and claims/aspirations/non-negotiables) are separated,
  never conflated, always source-attributed.
- Anti-references are captured, not just references.
- Business objective is explicit (as FACT, CLAIM, or flagged HYPOTHESIS —
  never silently omitted).
- No premature design/strategy/positioning content appears anywhere in the
  outputs.
- `DISCOVERY_GAPS.md` contains only decision-relevant gaps (materiality
  filter applied, not every unknown).

## Stop conditions
- **Normal completion:** all three required artifacts produced, every
  extracted statement classified into exactly one bucket, contradictions and
  material gaps logged (not resolved), `status: COMPLETE`.
- **Blocked (insufficient input):** source material is too sparse to
  establish even the business/offer/customer basics (e.g., a one-line
  description with nothing else) → `status: BLOCKED`, state exactly what
  additional material (interview, existing materials, even a short
  founder Q&A) is needed to proceed; still emit a minimal
  `BRAND_DISCOVERY_BRIEF.md` showing what little is known plus
  `DISCOVERY_GAPS.md` naming the blocking gaps, rather than nothing at all.
- **Never continue past:** stating a positioning decision, resolving a
  contradiction on the skill's own authority, or presenting a CLAIM/
  ASPIRATION as a verified FACT.
